# Review packet: Spanish (Spain), locale es-ES

Version 0.1. All content is synthetic.

## What this is

REIA is a software tool that helps real estate agents remember and understand their customer conversations. To test it we use invented chats and call notes. Every person, name, number and amount below is fictional.

The texts were drafted with AI assistance and checked with AI tools. Your review is meant to be the independent human check of the Spanish used in Spain.

## What we ask

- Read the three scenarios below (about 20 to 30 minutes, an estimate).
- Fill in `reviewer_feedback_template.md`. You can write in Spanish or English.
- Focus on: natural phrasing, regional language (Spain), real estate terminology, whether the customer's intent and objections are realistic, and factual consistency (numbers, dates, names, who says what).
- Suggest minimal corrections. You do not need to rewrite anything.
- Please do not include any real client information in your feedback.
- Taking part is optional and you can stop at any time.

Message references look like `ES-01 A3` (scenario, conversation, message).
